FROM DOCUMENT TO DECK

Make cards from PDF

💡

Free and local, with one limitation. This version extracts selectable text from a PDF in your browser and drafts cards using definitions, headings, lists and key sentences. It does not use an online AI model, so it won't understand every passage like a full AI tutor. Scanned/image-only PDFs need OCR and aren't supported in this simple version.

The PDF is processed on this device. Very large PDFs may take a little while. Always review the generated cards before studying.
